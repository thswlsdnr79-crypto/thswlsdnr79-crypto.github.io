English

TurtleTiles operates no developer server and does not collect your personal data.

  1. Photos — Photos you pick to create puzzles are processed and stored on your device only and are never transmitted to the developer or third parties.
  2. Game records — Moves and play time are stored only on your device.
  3. Usage analytics — Google Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics collect anonymized usage statistics (screens, buttons, device model, app version) and crash logs to improve the app. Your photos and puzzle content are never included. Data is transferred to and stored by Google LLC (US and other countries); retention up to 14 months.
  4. Payments — The optional "Support the Developer" purchase is processed by the Apple App Store; the developer cannot access your payment details.
  5. This app is not primarily directed at children and contains no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  6. Changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
