MoodMeleon

Track your daily mood as colors

MoodMeleon is an iOS app. Pick a color for how you feel, and it fills one cell of your calendar. Over a month you can see at a glance how your moods have flowed. You can also leave notes and drawings.

MoodMeleon uses Google Drive solely to back up the user's own diary data to a hidden, app-specific folder in their personal Drive, so the data can be restored after changing devices. It requests only the drive.appdata scope and never accesses any other file in the user's Drive.

English

Track your daily mood as colors

About

Pick a color for how you feel, and it fills one cell of your calendar. Over a month you can see at a glance how your moods have flowed. You can also leave notes and drawings.

Features

  • Mood colorsChoose from twelve mood colors and your calendar fills in.
  • Pixel artFill sample templates with mood colors to complete a picture.
  • StatisticsSee which moods dominated your month or year.
  • Google Drive backup (optional)Back up to an app-only folder in your own Google Drive so you can restore after changing devices. Only you and this app can reach that folder — the developer cannot.

About Google Drive backup

MoodMeleon offers Google Drive backup as an optional feature. It works only if you explicitly connect your own Google account.

  1. The app requests a single scope: drive.appdata. It grants access only to a hidden, app-specific folder created by this app.
  2. The app never reads or writes any other file in your Drive.
  3. Backups are stored in your own Drive. The developer has no access, and nothing is sent to a developer server.
  4. You can disconnect at any time in Settings.
  5. Every feature works without connecting an account.

Privacy

This app operates no developer server. See the privacy policy below for details.
