English

Your photos, reference images and individual scores never leave your device. Gecko Image sends only crash reports and anonymous usage counts.

  1. Photos — The reference image you choose and the photo you take are stored only inside the app's own storage on your device. They are never transmitted to the developer or to any third party.
  2. Scores and history — Similarity scores and session records are stored only on your device and are deleted with the app.
  3. Saving to your album — A result image is written to your photo library only when you tap save. The app requests add-only access and never reads your library. Choosing a reference image uses the system photo picker, which runs outside the app and requires no library permission.
  4. Crash reports and usage statistics — The app uses Firebase (Google) for two things: crash reports, so problems can be fixed, and anonymous usage counts — which screens were opened and which buttons were tapped, plus a score band (for example "good"). Also collected: your device model, OS version and a Firebase app instance ID that disappears when you delete the app. No photos, no reference images and no individual scores are ever sent, and nothing is linked to your identity — the app never sets a user ID and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). This is the app's only network use and its only third-party service. See Firebase's privacy information.
  5. Supporting the developer — The optional tip purchase is processed by the Apple App Store. The developer never sees your payment details, and nothing in the app is unlocked by it.
  6. No accounts, no ads, no tracking — The app has no sign-in, no advertising SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites. All image analysis runs on-device.
  7. This app is not directed at children.
  8. Changes to this policy will be announced on this page.

Informes de fallos y estadísticas de uso — La app usa Firebase (Google) para dos cosas: informes de fallos, para poder corregir problemas, y estadísticas anónimas de uso: qué pantallas se abrieron y qué botones se tocaron, además de un rango de puntuación (por ejemplo, «good»).
